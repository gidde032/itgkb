## Summary

Every major cloud store (Google Drive, OneDrive, Box) separates **personal** space owned by one
account from **shared/team** space owned by a group. The distinction is invisible day-to-day but
decides what happens when the owner leaves, hits a quota, or removes access — which is why it
causes the most "our files vanished" tickets.

## Diagnostic Steps

1. Ask where the files actually live: someone's "My Drive"/personal folder, or a shared/team
   drive. A folder someone *shared with you* is still owned by them.
2. Confirm the trigger: files disappeared after a person left, a share was removed, or an owner
   ran out of space — all point to personal ownership.

## Resolution Steps

1. For anything a team relies on, move it into a shared/team drive so the **team** owns it.
   Copying is not enough if the original stays personally owned — transfer or recreate in the
   team space.
2. If an owner already left and files are gone, recovery is an admin action (ownership transfer
   during offboarding) — escalate rather than asking the user to rebuild.

## Notes / Edge Cases

- "Shared with me" is a view of other people's files, not storage you own — teach users to copy
  important items into their own or the team's space.
- Offboarding is the classic failure: set the habit of team ownership *before* anyone leaves.
