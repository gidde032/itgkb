## Summary

Stub — the boundary between Service Center scope and central UMN IT (identity/account systems)
needs to be documented precisely, because getting it wrong wastes the user's time or touches
systems student employees shouldn't.

## To be documented

- Exactly which account actions are in-scope at the Service Center.
- Referral path and contact for central identity issues (resets, 2FA re-enrollment, lockouts).
- Verification requirements before discussing any account with a walk-in user.
