## Summary

Account access requests are a predictable high-frequency category. The critical knowledge is
the scope boundary: which actions a Carlson student employee performs directly, and which get
referred to central UMN IT (identity systems). Getting the boundary wrong either wastes the
user's time or touches systems the Service Center shouldn't.

## Common Scenarios

1. **Password reset** — universities standardly provide a self-service reset portal; the
   first move is directing the user there. [NEEDS VERIFICATION] — the UMN self-service reset
   URL, and whether any reset action is in-scope at the Service Center beyond directing to it.
2. **Two-factor problems** — new device enrollment, lost device, no backup codes.
   [NEEDS VERIFICATION] — confirm UMN's 2FA provider (Duo or otherwise) and which 2FA actions
   (e.g., re-enrollment) require central IT.
3. **Account lockout** — standard patterns are a cooldown wait or a central-IT unlock.
   [NEEDS VERIFICATION] — UMN's actual lockout policy and unlock path.
4. **New account provisioning** — [NEEDS VERIFICATION] — whether Carlson has any local
   provisioning procedure (new student employees, faculty) or if it is entirely central.
