## Summary

Intake quality determines how fast hardware support can act. The general checklist below is
standard practice; every Carlson-specific step is marked for verification.

## Intake Checklist

1. Record device type, make/model, and serial number. [NEEDS VERIFICATION] — whether Carlson
   asset-tags equipment and the tag format/location.
2. Record the user's description of the issue **verbatim** — paraphrasing loses symptoms.
3. Attempt to reproduce the issue before escalating; note exactly what you observed.
4. Visual inspection: photograph any physical damage at intake (protects both sides).
5. Log the intake. [NEEDS VERIFICATION] — TDX ticket, separate inventory system, or both.
6. Note data sensitivity if the device stores user data, before it changes hands.
