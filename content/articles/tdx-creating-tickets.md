## Summary

Creating and escalating TDX tickets is among the most common tasks in a Service Center shift.
The workflow shape: capture the issue → categorize → set priority → resolve in-tier or
escalate with documentation.

## Workflow

1. **Capture** — record the symptom in the user's words, plus environment (device, OS,
   browser) and exact error text. Searchable wording pays off later.
2. **Categorize** — issues arriving at the Service Center generally fall into: Google
   Workspace, account access, hardware, printing, software installation, and other UMN

   guidance for near-miss choices between categories.
3. **Set priority** — [NEEDS VERIFICATION] — Carlson priority definitions and any standing
   rules (e.g., whether certain requester types or outage classes auto-raise priority).
4. **Resolve or escalate** — attempt standard diagnostics for the category first. Transient
   issues get the wait-and-recheck treatment before any escalation (see the escalation
   article for the decision framework).
5. **Close well** — see the closure notes article for templates.
